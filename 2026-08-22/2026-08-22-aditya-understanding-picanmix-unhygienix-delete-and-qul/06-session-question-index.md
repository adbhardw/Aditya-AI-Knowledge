| Q3 | Why the difference, from first principles? | Delete strategy follows what the row is *for*. History-bearing rows (`partner_accounts`) → soft, because a missed filter fails **closed**. | Q5 | Is it `Scopes` vs `Unscoped` that decides soft vs hard? | **No — `Scopes` is irrelevant.** The rule is `hasDeletedAtField && !Unscoped`. Full truth table in the doc. | [02](02-gorm-v1-soft-delete-mechanism.md) §"Soft vs hard is `DeletedAt` × `Unscoped`" |
| Q6 | Is `question_user_lists` a different kind of export job? | **No — not a job at all.** `QUESTION_USER_LIST` is a *job type* and a **peer of `EXPORTS`** (`proto/domain.proto:45-51`). `question_user_list_details` is the sibling of `export_details`. | [03](03-question-user-list-vs-export-job-model.md) |
